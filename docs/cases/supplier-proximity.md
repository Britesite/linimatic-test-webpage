## Confirmed facts
- Customer: Ditlev Burke Industrial Tools A/S, Asnæs, Sjælland (industrial/hand-tool manufacturer).
- Project: a handle for a professional hand tool. Linimatic supplies the zinc die-cast part; Ditlev Burke now manufactures and pre-assembles the plastic part themselves.
- Result of the project: the entire handle is now assembled in Denmark, where it was previously assembled in Asia.
- Stated reasons for the partnership (from the customer, on record): Linimatic's automated production line is cost-competitive with Asian production; close cooperation; quality; reliability; faster in-country communication keeps projects moving; supports Ditlev Burke's philosophy of keeping jobs in Denmark.
- Quote source / contact: Camilla Burke Vedsmand, Managing Director (Administrerende direktør), Ditlev Burke Industrial Tools A/S — quoted by name and title in the original article.
- Article was signed by Jan V. Jørgensen (Linimatic), who is also the current session's requester.
